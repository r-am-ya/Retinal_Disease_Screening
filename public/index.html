04 / STUDY DESIGN

What was trained, and how?

DATASET—

Sharmin et al. 2024 (Data in Brief 57, 110979), nine fundus classes. The external-eye pterygium class was excluded. Augmented image files were not used because they could not be reliably linked back to their source images, which would risk leakage.

SPLIT—

Stratified by class with seed 42. Exact perceptual-hash duplicate clusters stayed in one partition. Validation and test contain original images only.

MODEL—

—

LIMITATIONSNot patient-level

Patient IDs were unavailable, so patient-level leakage cannot be excluded. Data come from two hospitals in one country, rare classes have very few test images, and no external validation has been done. Not for clinical use.

Classes included

Central serous chorioretinopathy, diabetic retinopathy, disc edema, glaucoma, healthy, macular scar, myopia, retinal detachment and retinitis pigmentosa.
